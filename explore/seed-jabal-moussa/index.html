Jabal Moussa Biosphere Reserve

Jabal Moussa is a UNESCO-listed Man and the Biosphere reserve that straddles the hills near Faqra and Mayrouba. It's more nature and eco-tourism than historical sightseeing, but it's commonly paired with these villages in itineraries — marked hiking trails, forest and river landscapes, and some of the best-preserved natural terrain in the wider Faraya region.
